# Research notes and source guide

This file helps the group understand the sources cited in the report. The first two are the two main related attendance-system studies required by the assignment.

## Main related study 1

**Liew, K.J. and Tan, T.H. (2021), “QR Code-Based Student Attendance System.”** Proceedings of the 2021 2nd Asia Conference on Computers and Communications (ACCC 2021), pp. 10-14. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCC54619.2021.00009

What the study reports: The authors describe a mobile QR attendance app tested by students at Sunway University. Before recording attendance, the system checked the class hour, registered mobile device and geolocation.

How it relates to this project: It shows why a QR scan should be validated against the proper student and active class session. The project proposes individual sign-in, course/session validation, a time limit and duplicate checking. The group should not assume that collecting location data is necessary; local privacy rules and user feedback must guide that decision.

Publisher/institution record: https://scholarprofiles.sunwayuniversity.edu.my/en/publications/qr-code-based-student-attendance-system/

## Main related study 2

**Hamdani, D., Wibowo, A.P.W. and Heryono, H. (2025), “Evaluating QR Code-Based Attendance System Using User Experience.”** Applied Information System and Management, 8(2), pp. 277-284. DOI: https://doi.org/10.15408/aism.v8i2.46679

What the study reports: The researchers studied a mobile attendance application called Attend. The evaluation involved 35 student respondents and included interviews, focus group discussions, task testing using Maze and a User Experience Questionnaire. The article reports efficient task completion, while noting that some interface components needed refinement.

How it relates to this project: It supports involving students and collecting feedback while designing and evaluating the interface. The project therefore includes a questionnaire, lecturer interviews and task-based usability tests rather than relying only on the group's personal opinion about the screens.

Article page: https://journal.uinjkt.ac.id/index.php/aism/article/view/46679

## Additional design guidance

**National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), Human Centered Design (HCD).** Updated 27 May 2026. https://www.nist.gov/itl/iad/human-centered-technologies/human-factors-human-centered-design

The page describes understanding the context of use, specifying user requirements, producing design solutions and evaluating the design. It also emphasizes user involvement and iterative refinement. This is the basis for the proposed methodology in Section 4.

**International Organization for Standardization (ISO) (2018), ISO 9241-11:2018 - Ergonomics of human-system interaction - Part 11: Usability: Definitions and concepts.** https://www.iso.org/standard/63500.html

This standard provides a framework for understanding and applying usability to interactive systems and products. It supports the report's focus on how effectively and satisfactorily users can complete attendance tasks.
